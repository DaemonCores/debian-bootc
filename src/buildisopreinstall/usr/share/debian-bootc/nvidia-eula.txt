**NVIDIA driver acceptance (NVIDIA Software License Agreement, driver and
CUDA user-space).** By installing the `-ia` image you accept the NVIDIA
Software License Agreement for the NVIDIA driver user-space libraries and
CUDA components shipped in this image. The kernel modules themselves are
the MIT-licensed open-gpu-kernel-modules, built in CI as a plan deb
against the project kernel — nothing compiles on this machine.
Redistributing the image without the acceptance step at installation is
not permitted. The complete agreement is reproduced at first install and
by `nvidia-smi --license` on the installed host; the authoritative text:
<https://www.nvidia.com/en-us/drivers/nvidia-license/>. The full verbatim
agreement is pinned here before the first public `-ia` build — PENDING,
installer lane.
